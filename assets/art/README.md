## Money plant prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent decorative botanical asset for a personal portfolio website. Paint a single long trailing pothos / money plant vine, hanging down from upper left, looping gently down and right, heart-shaped variegated leaves in forest green #1F4D3A, muted moss #14352A, sage #A9BBA0 and leaf #6E8B4E. Hand painted opaque gouache, dry visible brushwork, textured irregular leaf edges, earthy gentle Studio Ghibli background-art feeling. Vertical composition about 1:2, branching leaf clusters with plenty of transparent air between them, all leaves fully within frame. Actual transparent background. No pot, no wall, no paper rectangle, no text, no shadow backdrop. This will be placed along the margins of a paper website. Leaves should be relatively large and individually legible rather than tiny detailed foliage.
